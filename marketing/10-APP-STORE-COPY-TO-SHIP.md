
(91 / 100. Don't repeat words already in the title or subtitle — Apple penalizes that. We avoided "audio," "lock-in," "deep," "focus" duplication.)

**Keyword rationale:**
- `focus`, `study`, `sound`, `ambient` — primary high-intent.
- `rain`, `coffee`, `lofi`, `jazz` — channel-level long-tail.
- `white`, `noise` — dominant search head term, indirect match.
- `sleep`, `relax` — secondary use cases.
- `deep`, `work`, `locked`, `in` — let Apple combine these into "deep work" and "locked in" matches via algorithmic phrase building.
