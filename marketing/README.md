## The thesis in one sentence

> Gen Z and millennial knowledge-workers are publicly committing to a "lock-in" identity in 2026 — Elseware becomes the *audio uniform* of that identity.

## The math we have to hit

- **Day 30:** 5,000 IG followers, 25k cumulative Reel views, 500 installs, 100 paid unlocks (~$700 net at $9.99 unlock).
- **Day 60:** 25,000 followers, 1 viral Reel (>500k views), 2,500 installs, 500 paid unlocks.
- **Day 90:** 75,000 followers, 10,000 installs, 2,000 paid unlocks (~$14k net).
- **Day 180:** 250,000 followers, profitable monthly without paid acquisition.

These are not gentle goals. They are the floor. See `08-KPIS-AND-DECISIONS.md` for how we kill what doesn't work and triple down on what does.

## The non-negotiables

1. **One Reel a day, every day.** No exceptions. Not "4–7 a week." Daily. (See ops playbook for batch days.)
2. **Saves and shares > likes.** We optimize for save/share rate. Likes are vanity.
3. **DM-driven distribution.** Every Reel ends with a save-able or shareable hook because IG 2026 weights DM-sends as the #1 distribution signal.
4. **Originality score protected.** Zero recycled content. Zero TikTok watermarks. Zero stolen audio.
5. **Brand consistency wins.** Same warm-cream palette (#f4f1ec), same "Elseware" wordmark, same calm-but-disciplined voice across every asset.
6. **Ship the offer. Update the App Store.** The plan is worthless if the offer in the app doesn't match what we're selling on IG.

## What changes if this works

- Elseware becomes the iOS audio app for "locking in," not the 47th ambient-sound app on the App Store.
- The IG account itself becomes a media asset worth 6 figures — we can spin off merch, presets-as-products, a paid Discord, a yearly cohort.
- We have leverage: future apps from the same studio launch into a warm, addicted audience.

## What happens if this fails

- We learn faster than 99% of indie devs whether the "lock-in" wedge converts.
- The content asset (Reels, captions, hooks) compounds even at low growth.
- Worst-case: we have a documented system another team can pick up tomorrow.
