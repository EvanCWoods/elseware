# 14 — Twitter/X Strategy (founder-account play)

> Instagram is the brand. X is the founder. Don't confuse them.

This doc adds X (formerly Twitter) as a distribution channel — but only on terms that don't break the IG-first plan. **If you can't sustain IG, do not start on X.** Read `06-DAILY-OPS-PLAYBOOK.md` first; this doc layers on top.

---

## The first decision — brand account or founder account?

**Pick founder account. Not the brand.**

Why:
- **X rewards specificity and personality.** A brand account on X reads as marketing in a place where people don't want marketing. A founder voice — "I just shipped X today" — earns ambient distribution from the indie-builder ecosystem (Pieter Levels, Marc Lou, Tony Dinh, Anne-Laure Le Cunff, et al.).
- **Lower posting bar.** Founder accounts can post a 20-word thought; brand accounts feel obligated to be on-brand and polished, which slows velocity.
- **Cross-pollination.** Mention `@elseware` on IG; mention "I made elseware" on X. The audiences barely overlap, so the combined surface is genuinely additive.

So: keep `@elseware` on IG as the brand. Use **your personal X** (or a clearly personal handle) for the X play. If you don't have an X account, claim one with a real-name-ish handle — *not* "@elsewareceo" or similar.

---

## Does this fit your time budget?

Per `06-DAILY-OPS-PLAYBOOK.md` you're already doing 14 hrs/wk on IG operations.

X budget: **~3 additional hrs/wk** (5–10 short tweets + 1 weekly thread + 30 min replies).

**Hard rule:** if any week you can't ship the IG cadence, **skip X that week**. X is the addition; IG is the foundation. Founder accounts forgive a quiet week. Brand accounts don't.

If 14 + 3 = 17 hrs/wk is too much: **don't start X.** Re-read this doc when you've stabilized IG at 8 weeks in.

## The four pillars on X

### Pillar 1 — Build-in-public ledger (40% of output)

The audience X rewards most is the audience that compounds for indie builders: other indie builders. They follow you for the *real numbers* and *real decisions*.

This pillar is mechanical:
- Weekly **Friday thread** tied to the public dashboard at `elseware.app/launch-status`. Pull the same numbers `npm run friday` updates: cumulative unlocks, founders count, ladder progress. Add 1–2 lessons.
- Mid-week tweet: "shipped the focus timer today. it's gated to lifetime owners. screenshot below."
- One-line revenue/install observations as they happen: "first 100 unlocks. mostly from the lisbon rainstorm reel."

Voice: factual, dry, no humble-bragging, no "I'm so grateful." Numbers > narratives.

### Pillar 2 — Lock-in philosophy (25% of output)

Single-sentence aphorisms in the same brand voice as the IG. These are *quotable*:
- "discipline is just the right ambient."
- "subscriptions are for renting your own discipline."
- "silence is a distraction. so is the wrong audio."
- "the audio is the lock. the phone is the leak."
- "first 90 minutes of the day belong to the work."

These are the X equivalent of Pillar 1 (Lock-In Memes) on IG. They get screenshot-shared and quoted.

### Pillar 3 — Behind-the-scenes craft (20% of output)

Show the work. The kind of thing that doesn't fit on IG.
- "spent 2 hours on the soft serif italic in the icon variant. variant b is going up against the control next month."
- "rewriting the paywall copy this morning. dropped 'PRO' entirely. nothing in elseware will ever be called 'pro'."
- A photo of a notebook with a soundscape sketch.

Voice: process-oriented, occasionally vulnerable, never humble-bragging.

### Pillar 4 — Replies to indie builders (15% of output)

Don't reply to celebrities. Don't reply to mega-accounts. Reply substantively to **mid-tier indie builders** (1k–50k followers) when their post is on-topic.

Two rules:
1. The reply must add information, not affirm. "Same — we're at 300 unlocks two weeks in" beats "this is so true!!"
2. Don't reply with a link to elseware unless asked.

This is how you land on builders' radar without spamming them. Over 3–6 months, half your X followers come from these replies, not your tweets.

## Cadence

| Day | Time | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | morning | 1 short tweet (Pillar 2 or 3) |
| Tue | morning | 1 short tweet (Pillar 2 or 3) |
| Wed | morning | 1 short tweet (Pillar 1 — mid-week ship update) |
| Thu | morning | 1 short tweet (Pillar 2 or 3) |
| Fri | 9 AM | **The weekly thread** (Pillar 1 — see template below) |
| Sat | morning | 1 short tweet, optional (Pillar 2 or 3) |
| Sun | none | quiet day |
| Daily | 10 min | reply hour — 5–10 substantive replies to indie builders |

That's ~5–7 tweets/wk + 1 thread + ~50 replies. Total: 3 hrs/wk.

## The Friday thread template (copy-paste, fill in)

```
week N of elseware. shipped publicly at elseware.app/launch-status

→ [N] lifetime unlocks (was [N-prev] last week)
→ [N] founders on the wall
→ [main thing shipped this week]
→ [main thing learned this week]

→ next week: [the one thing]

screenshot of dashboard below ↓
```

Then attach a screenshot of `elseware.app/launch-status` and the tweet links to it.

This thread becomes a perpetual distribution mechanism. Each week's thread links to the public dashboard, which is the conversion surface. Indie builders quote-tweet weekly progress. Quarterly drops become bigger threads with more detail.

## What X-specific hooks look like (different from IG)

X rewards **declarative statements** more than IG's POV/Reel hooks. Templates that work in our voice:

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Claim | "the only audio i've found that consistently produces flow state is hand-built ambient." |
| Counter | "study spotify playlists are the worst possible focus tool. lyrics steal cycles you need." |
| Observation | "subscription apps for focus is one of the strangest categories. you rent your own discipline." |
| Number | "first 100 lifetime unlocks closed in 4 days. 80% came from one reel about lisbon rainstorm." |
| Confession | "i originally wanted to make this a subscription. spent 3 months convincing myself it was lazy." |

Write 3 a day in your notes app. Ship the best one. Delete the rest.

## Cross-channel rules (so we don't dilute either)

1. **Never repost IG content verbatim.** Format for X: shorter, more declarative, no emoji-as-anchor. The same idea can land in both places, but rewritten.
2. **X gets earlier access to thinking.** New presets, brand decisions, philosophy — show on X first as drafts. The polished version goes to IG once it's locked.
3. **IG gets the consumer; X gets the maker.** Don't try to convert X users into the same tier of fan. Different audiences, different relationships.
4. **The Friday thread mirrors the Friday IG Story but isn't the same content.** IG Story is a 10-second animated counter; X thread is 5 lines of text with the dashboard screenshot.
5. **The personal X account stays personal forever.** It's not a brand asset. It's *your* account. Year 5, when you've shipped the third app, this account will be your most valuable distribution surface.

---

## What X is NOT for

These are the most common ways indie founders waste their X time:
- ❌ Replying to celebrities or mega-accounts hoping for retweet luck.
- ❌ Fake-deep manifestos about the future of work.
- ❌ Threads about "how I made $X in Y days" (cringe; spike for a day, decline forever).
- ❌ Begging for retweets / "please boost."
- ❌ "Follow for follow" loops.
- ❌ Selling courses about how you did it (you haven't done it yet).
- ❌ Day-long political fights.
- ❌ Live-tweeting tech announcements.

If you find yourself doing any of these: stop, get off the app, walk for 20 minutes.

## The decision rules

### When to start (Day 30 of IG cadence at the earliest)

Do not start X until:
1. You've shipped IG every day for 30 consecutive days.
2. You have a public dashboard live with real numbers.
3. You have at least one piece of content you'd be proud to lead with.

### When to scale (Day 90 / 1k X followers)

If at Day 90 you have:
- 1,000+ X followers organically.
- 5+ tweets that crossed 10k impressions.
- Visible referral traffic to `elseware.app/links` from X.

…then **double the cadence** for 30 days (10–14 tweets/wk + 2 threads/wk). After 30 days, settle on whatever sustainable rate produced the best return.

### When to kill or pause (Day 60 of X with <300 followers)

If at Day 60 of X cadence you have <300 followers and no measurable referral traffic to the App Store or `elseware.app/links` from X: **pause for 60 days.** Revisit when you have a quarterly drop or a viral IG Reel — you'll have the warmest material. Don't grind the channel cold.

X is the only channel in this entire plan that it's *acceptable* to pause. IG is non-negotiable. X is leveraged, optional, additive.

## How X feeds back into the rest of the plan

A working X account changes the math on three things in `13-POST-LAUNCH-PLAYBOOK.md`:

1. **Press push (Phase 7.5)** — journalists check X first. A consistent X presence with real numbers is itself the press hook.
2. **Influencer test (Phase 7.4)** — indie creators on X are easier to recruit than IG creators because they engage in DM more openly. Many of our best collabs will come from X-discovered creators we approach with a comp Lifetime Unlock + a request to make whatever they want.
3. **Second-app pre-launch list (Phase 9.1)** — X followers convert at higher rates than IG followers for "I'm shipping a new thing" announcements. The second app launches into a warm X audience, not a cold one.

X's real ROI lives in those three downstream effects, not in the day-to-day tweets.

## What we don't ship on X

Same rules as `01-POSITIONING-AND-ICP.md`. Same words we never use. Same voice rules. The fact that X is the founder voice doesn't mean we drop the brand — it means we filter the brand through a single human's perspective.

The personal X account *can* talk about things that aren't elseware-related. But:
- ~70% of posts should ladder back to elseware or the lock-in philosophy.
- ~30% can be about other things (programming, design, books, music) — but only if they're consistent with the same calm-disciplined voice. No politics. No hot takes. No "I'm so blessed" energy.

## The single decision rule for every X tweet

> *"Would the founder I admire most retweet this without explanation?"*

If yes, ship. If no, save it for the notes app.

The founder you admire is whoever you'd most want to have coffee with. Anne-Laure Le Cunff. Tony Dinh. Marc Lou. David Perell. Pieter Levels. Whoever the list is. They all have one thing in common: the bar is *substantive*. Aphorisms that are also honest. Numbers that are real. Replies that add info.

That bar killed 90% of my drafts before they shipped. That's the point.

## Compounding on X (year 1 → year 5)

By Year 5 the X account is the single most valuable distribution surface you own. Why:

- Twitter followers move with you across products. IG followers anchor to the `@elseware` brand and don't follow you to a new project as easily.
- Builders on X are the most likely to write about you, fund you, hire you, recommend you.
- The 4-year compound on a quiet, on-brand X account is bigger than the 4-year compound on an IG brand account, because X rewards *time and consistency* over *aesthetic batching*.

Year 5 X follower count target: 50k+. Year 5 X account value: incalculable, but if forced — easily $250k for a sale of the account if such a thing were sold (it's not).

Plant this seed at week 4. Water it for 5 years. Don't pivot, don't burn out, don't take vacations from it longer than 14 days.
