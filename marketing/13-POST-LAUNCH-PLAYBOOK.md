# 13 — Post-Launch Playbook (Days 91–365)

> The 90-day plan got you a brand. The next 270 days turn it into a business.

This doc picks up exactly where `04-90-DAY-CONTENT-CALENDAR.md` ends. The 90-day plan was about *getting to break-even*. This is about *compounding the asset.*

If you're reading this, you've hit (or are within sight of) the Day-90 floor: ~75k IG followers, ~10k installs, ~2k paid unlocks. The asset is real. Now we use it.

---

## Phase 7 — Days 91–180: Layer paid on what's working

**Goal:** scale paid acquisition without changing the offer, the brand, or the daily content cadence. Add fuel; don't change the engine.

### 7.1 — When to start paid

Trigger: **after at least one Reel has crossed 500k organic views AND save rate >5% on 5+ Reels.** Below that threshold, paid amplifies a brand that doesn't yet convert. You'd burn money confirming the wrong thing.

If you haven't hit the trigger by Day 90: extend organic for another 30 days. **Do not rescue with money.** That's the rule from `08-KPIS-AND-DECISIONS.md`. It still applies.

### 7.2 — The three campaigns we test (Day 91 → Day 105)

Total budget: $60/day = $840 over 14 days. Same numbers as `07-FUNNEL-AND-ASO.md` Step 10.

**Campaign 1 — Boost organic winners (Reels Ads).**
- Pick the top 3 Reels by save rate from Days 1–90.
- Promote each via Meta Ads Manager → Reels placement only.
- Bid: lowest-cost. Daily budget: $20/Reel.
- Audience: Lookalike based on people who've engaged with `@elseware` posts in the last 60 days.
- Goal: $5–7 CAC per install. Validate by Day 105.

**Campaign 2 — TikTok Spark Ads.**
- Boost the *same* top 3 Reels on TikTok via Spark Ads (you "spark" your own organic post; same creative, paid distribution).
- Daily budget: $20 total.
- Goal: validate that the Reel's hook works on TikTok at scale. If TikTok CAC is under $5, double the budget on Day 106.

**Campaign 3 — Apple Search Ads (long-tail).**
- Bid on: `audio for locking in`, `study sound mixer one time purchase`, `sounds like a tokyo cafe`, `ambient mixer no subscription`.
- Bid: $1.50 max.
- Daily budget: $20.
- Goal: validate App Store conversion at non-brand keywords. Sub-$5 CAC = winner.

### 7.3 — Kill rules for paid

- Any campaign over $7 CAC by Day 14 → kill.
- Any campaign under $5 CAC by Day 14 → 2× the budget.
- Any campaign between $5 and $7 CAC → run for 30 more days, then re-evaluate.

> Honest math: $7 net contribution per unlock means $7 CAC = breakeven. Anything below that is profit; anything above is a brand-awareness cost we can pay only if organic is healthy.

### 7.4 — Influencer test (Day 120)

One test. One creator. Don't spray.

- **Find the creator:** mid-tier study/aesthetic IG (10k–100k followers) whose audience overlaps with our ICP. Check engagement rate (>4% on Reels), save rate visible in their public posts, and whether their voice is on-brand. Aesthetic-coded > follower-count.
- **The pitch:** DM template, lowercase, calm — *"hi — we made elseware. it's a one-purchase ambient sound mixer for locking in. no subscription. would you genuinely use it? if yes, here's a free Lifetime Unlock + a comp partnership: $X for one Reel + one Story per month for 3 months, ungated. content is yours, no scripts, no approvals."*
- **Comp:** $300–$1,000 per month for 3 months. The creator picks the Reel format. We do not script, do not gate, do not require disclosure language beyond what FTC requires.
- **Decision rule:** if it doesn't drive 50+ tracked installs in month 1, pause. If it does, expand to 3 creators in month 2.

### 7.5 — Press push (Day 130, only if 25k+ followers + 1 viral Reel)

Per `marketing/07-FUNNEL-AND-ASO.md` Step 9. Compressed:

- **Indie newsletters** (Indie Hackers, Lenny's Newsletter, Ben's Bites). Personal pitch, 100 words, no PR firm. We send `marketing/00-EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md` as the angle and offer one journalist a free Lifetime + an interview.
- **iOS reviewers on YouTube** (MacStories, MacRumors, AppleInsider, Marques Brownlee's iOS list).
- **Apple App Store Editorial** — pitch via App Store Connect → "Pitch Your App." Aesthetic-first apps win regularly. The pitch leans on (1) the place metaphor, (2) the lifetime model in a subscription world, (3) the 6 hand-built places as content design.

We pitch *once*. We don't follow up more than once. We move on.

### 7.6 — The Day-180 outcome (the floor)

If Phase 7 worked:
- 250k+ IG followers.
- 25k+ installs cumulative.
- 5k+ paid unlocks cumulative (= ~$35k net revenue).
- Paid CAC under $7.
- Cumulative ad spend under 30% of total revenue.
- The "founders wall closes at 5,000" milestone has happened (or is close).

If you're below those numbers, **don't chase by spending more.** Re-read `marketing/08-KPIS-AND-DECISIONS.md`. The diagnosis is one of: ICP wrong, content wrong, offer wrong, product wrong. One of those four. Fix that — don't add fuel to a leaking engine.

## Phase 8 — Days 181–270: Compound the brand

**Goal:** turn audience-only growth into a business with three legs. Each new leg uses the existing audience; we don't fragment.

### 8.1 — The Founders Club (Day 200)

Lifetime owners are an asset, not a transaction. Activate them.

- **Private channel.** A Discord or Geneva (whichever feels less default). Invite-only, gated by Lifetime ownership (verified via the Lifetime owner's email at signup).
- **Cadence.** One async studio update per week (founder voice memo or 200-word note). Monthly "lock-in challenge" (90 minutes, all owners working in parallel, captured to public Reel).
- **What it isn't.** Not a community manager job. Not Slack. Not a Discourse forum. We don't engage daily. The Club is a quiet room for owners to know each other exists.
- **Why it matters.** Reduces churn, generates UGC, makes the brand feel collective, gives us a warm list for future products.

### 8.2 — Quarterly drops as content engine (Day 210, recurring)

Every preset drop is a 14-day content event:

- **Day -7:** teaser Reel. "first listen: copenhagen apartment."
- **Day -3:** founder story Reel. "the story of copenhagen apartment — what it was made for."
- **Day 0:** drop Reel. "live now in the app. free for lifetime owners."
- **Day +7:** UGC repost compilation. "your reactions to copenhagen."
- **Day +10:** ranked carousel. "all 8 places, ranked by what you're locking in for."
- **Day +14:** retrospective Story. "copenhagen by the numbers — N first-listens, M new lifetimes."

Drops keep the brand from getting stale. They're also free press hooks (one product update = one journalist email). Every drop earns 2–4 weeks of organic Reels without touching the script bank.

### 8.3 — Merch (Day 240, only if margin works)

Two SKUs. Maximum.

1. **The Lock-In Notebook** — A5 or B6, soft serif "elseware" wordmark on the cover, 90 ruled pages (one per day for a quarter). $24. Print-on-demand via Printify or similar. Margin: ~$10.
2. **The Lock-In Sticker Pack** — 3 stickers: the wordmark, "lock in.", and the "e" glyph from the icon variant. $6. Sticker Mule. Margin: ~$4.

Sell on `elseware.app/shop` (new page). Ship from a single PO box. No customer service complexity. No T-shirts (size returns kill the margin).

**Decision rule:** if we don't sell 100 notebooks in the first 30 days, kill the merch line. Not every brand should sell physical goods. We're an audio app first.

### 8.4 — The annual cohort (Day 270, the speculative bet)

Hormozi's "container word" plays here. We launch:

> **The Lock-In Cohort** — 30 days, $99, January only. Daily lock-in protocol, weekly group video call, private channel just for the cohort, custom soundscape made for the group.

Why it works:
- Higher price point than the app ($99 vs $9.99) on the same audience.
- Time-limited — only runs once a year (honest scarcity).
- Capped at 100 people year one, 250 year two. Real ceiling.
- Generates a year of testimonials we can use forever.

**Decision rule:** if we don't sell out the first cohort in 7 days at $99 / 100-person cap = $9,900 revenue, the cohort idea is wrong for this audience. Don't run a second one. Refund and move on.

If it sells out: the path to $500k+ ARR runs through annual cohorts, not subscription bolts to the app.

## Phase 9 — Days 271–365: The year-one moat

**Goal:** by Day 365, Block Software is a studio with a warm audience, not just an app with users.

### 9.1 — The second app (Day 280, the leverage move)

The whole point of the lifetime model + IG audience compound is that the **second app** launches into a warm crowd we already own.

What it should be:
- Same brand voice, same visual system, same lifetime price model.
- Solves a different "lock-in" adjacent problem. Examples: a sleep-soundscape app (Tulum at midnight, Lisbon thunderstorm at 2am — same place metaphor, sleep mode); a movement-sounds app (Trans-Siberian for runs, Bali Morning for walks); a breathwork app (single-place, 90-minute sessions, paced breath cues over our existing audio).
- Not Calm, not Headspace. We are not building a "wellness platform." Each app does one thing.
- Same one-time-purchase economics. Bundle option: "All Block Software apps lifetime — $24.99."

**Pre-launch list:** every Lifetime owner on the founders wall + Founders Club gets early access. We DM them all. The second app launches with 1,000+ pre-installs.

### 9.2 — The annual report (Day 365, the brand asset)

A single web page at `elseware.app/year-one`. Public.

What's on it:
- Cumulative numbers (total unlocks, total Reel views, total saves, total founders, App Store rating, App Store rank progression).
- The 5 Reels that mattered most (link to each).
- Lessons learned (3–5 items, blunt).
- What's next (the second app, the next cohort, the next quarter's drop).
- Thanks list — every founder by handle (already on the wall, but featured here too).

This page becomes the founding artifact of the studio. It's also the single best inbound recruiting / pitching artifact we'll ever have. It's how we'll talk about Year 1 forever.

### 9.3 — Retrospective + replan

Same week as the annual report. Run a one-day retro:

- What worked → keep.
- What didn't → cut.
- What surprised us → understand.
- Year 2 plan: 4 more drops, 1 cohort, 1 second app, target 10× revenue.

Update every doc in `marketing/` with the new floor numbers and what we learned. The plan becomes a lived document.

## Phase 10 — Year 2+ (the studio playbook)

By the end of Year 1 the brand is real. Year 2's leverage points:

1. **Block Software bundle.** "All apps lifetime, $24.99." Cross-sell on every app's paywall.
2. **Two cohorts a year.** January and June. $99 → $149 → $199 as we get oversubscribed.
3. **Quarterly drops permanently.** They're the content engine.
4. **A third app.** Same pattern.
5. **Hire one person.** A part-time editor at $1k/mo to free 5 hrs/week of operator time. This is the first hire because content velocity is the moat.
6. **Press once a year.** End-of-year recap is the natural press hook. Don't pitch monthly.
7. **No VC money.** A $50–500k/yr indie studio is the goal. Subscription venture math doesn't apply to lifetime products.

## What we don't do post-launch (still)

These rules from `01-POSITIONING-AND-ICP.md` and `02-GRAND-SLAM-OFFER.md` get *more* important after Day 90, not less. Re-read them every quarter.

- ❌ Discount the app. Ever.
- ❌ Add a subscription tier. Ever.
- ❌ Send a newsletter.
- ❌ Build a "wellness platform."
- ❌ Hire community managers to engagement-bait.
- ❌ Run sales for major holidays.
- ❌ Add tracking pixels.
- ❌ Take VC money for an app this category.
- ❌ Pivot to enterprise.
- ❌ Pivot at all in Year 1. The plan is the plan.

## The single decision rule for every Year-1 question

> *"Does this make a locked-in 23-year-old proud to own Elseware?"*

Yes → ship it. No → don't.

Apply to every feature, every collaboration, every Reel format, every post, every email, every line of merch, every cohort positioning, every thought.

The brand survives the next 10 years if and only if this question gets a clean Yes every quarter. Most apps die because they let too many "kind of" answers stack up.
