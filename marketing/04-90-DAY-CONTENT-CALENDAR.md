# 04 — The 90-Day Ruthless Content Calendar

> Plans get abandoned at the daily-decision level. So we made every daily decision in advance.

This calendar covers **Day 1 through Day 90.** Each day has a specific Reel format, hook seed, on-screen text seed, caption seed, and CTA. The Reel scripts themselves live in `05-REELS-SCRIPT-BANK.md`. This file is the *order* in which to ship them and the *theme* for that day.

**Start date:** Today + 7 days. Use Days -7 to -1 for the launch-week prep (`09-LAUNCH-WEEK.md`). Day 1 = first public post.

---

## How to read this calendar

Each row in the daily plans is structured:

```
DAY N  |  Pillar  |  Format        |  Hook seed                                |  Caption seed                                |  CTA
```

**Pillars:**
- `P1` = Lock-In Meme (40% of output)
- `P2` = Soundscape POV (35% of output)
- `P3` = Place Drop / Aesthetic (25% of output)

**Formats:**
- `Reel` = 9:16 vertical, 7–22s
- `Long-Reel` = 9:16, 45–60s, narrative
- `Carousel` = 4:5, 6 slides
- `Story` = 9:16, 24h ephemeral
- `Live` = once a month, 30min studio session

## Week-level rhythm (so the calendar is repeatable forever)

- **Mon:** Hero Reel — Pillar 2 (Soundscape POV). Drives installs.
- **Tue:** Pillar 1 meme. Drives saves and shares.
- **Wed:** Pillar 1 meme OR UGC repost. Drives community signal.
- **Thu:** Hero Reel — Pillar 2. Drives installs.
- **Fri:** Pillar 1 meme. Drives weekend saves.
- **Sat:** Pillar 3 (aesthetic / place drop). Drives saves and Story shares.
- **Sun:** Long-Reel (45–60s). Narrative or recap. Drives followers.

Two carousels per week (Mon evening, Thu evening). Stories every day. Live once a month.

---

## What's missing from the calendar (deliberately)

We do *not* schedule:
- Trending audio Reels (because we should be *making* the trending audio, not riding it).
- "Day in the life" of the founder (off-brand for now; revisit in Month 6).
- Influencer collabs (we don't have product-market-fit data yet; defer to Month 4+).
- Paid promotion (Month 4 only, after we know what organic content converts).
- App Store editorial pitches (Month 2; see ASO doc).

These are *future levers*, not *Month 1–3 levers*. Discipline is half the plan.

---

## Replacement rules (what to do when something works or fails)

- If a Reel hits >5% save rate by view 1,000 → we *clone it 5 ways* in the next 7 days.
- If a Reel hits <2% save rate by view 1,000 → we *kill that format* for 14 days, then revisit only if we have a stronger hook.
- If a single Reel goes viral (>500k views in <72hrs) → we *clear the calendar for 3 days* and ship 5 same-format Reels back-to-back while the algo is hot.
- If a place drop underperforms → we *do not* discount or extend. We let it run, learn, and ship the next place.
