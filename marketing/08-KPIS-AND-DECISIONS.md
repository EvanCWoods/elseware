# 08 — KPIs & Decision Rules

> Plans without kill-rules become wishlists. Every metric below has a number that says: stop, fix, or scale.

---

## The 7 metrics that matter

We track exactly 7. Anything else is noise.

### 1. Save rate (per Reel) — the leading indicator

`save_rate = saves / views` measured at the 24-hour mark.

| State | Range | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| **Floor (kill)** | <2% | Format dies for 14 days. Revisit with a stronger hook. |
| **Median (continue)** | 2–4% | Keep, refine hook. |
| **Healthy** | 4–7% | Keep, clone same format weekly. |
| **Hit (scale)** | 7%+ | Clone 5 ways in next 7 days. Pin to profile. |

Saves are the single best predictor of long-term reach in Reels 2026. Optimize for this above all.

### 2. Share rate (per Reel) — the secondary leading indicator

`share_rate = shares / views` at 24h.

| State | Range | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| **Floor** | <0.8% | Format dies. |
| **Median** | 0.8–2% | Continue. |
| **Healthy** | 2–4% | Pin format. |
| **Hit** | 4%+ | Clear calendar. Ship 5 same-format Reels in 7 days. |

DM-shares are the #1 algorithmic signal in 2026 IG. We optimize CTAs ("save & send to your study friend") to drive this.

### 3. Follower growth rate — the trust indicator

`fgr = followers_gained_today / total_followers_yesterday` × 100

| State | Range | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| **Floor** | <0.5%/day | Audit profile presentation; first 9 grid posts may be off. |
| **Median** | 0.5–2%/day | Continue. |
| **Healthy** | 2–5%/day | We're compounding. |
| **Hit** | >5%/day | We have a viral Reel. Capitalize. |

Note: percentage matters more than absolute number, especially in the first 30 days when the base is small.

### 4. Bio link click-through rate — the funnel-bridge metric

`btr = bio_clicks / profile_visits`

| State | Range | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| **Floor** | <2% | Bio copy or pic is failing. Test a new bio next week. |
| **Median** | 2–5% | Continue. |
| **Healthy** | 5–10% | Profile is converting. |
| **Hit** | >10% | Pin a different Reel as featured to test if we can replicate. |

### 5. Install-to-unlock rate — the product-conversion metric

`iur = paid_unlocks / installs` over a rolling 14-day cohort.

| State | Range | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| **Floor** | <8% | Paywall positioning is wrong; A/B test new triggers. |
| **Median** | 8–15% | Continue. |
| **Healthy** | 15–25% | Strong product-market fit signal. |
| **Hit** | >25% | We've nailed the trigger. Don't change anything. |

Mobile app install-to-paid benchmarks are 2–5% for subscriptions. Lifetime should outperform; floor of 8% is realistic.

### 6. End-to-end Reel-to-unlock rate — the ROI metric

`e2e = unlocks_attributed / views` over rolling 14-day cohort.

| State | Range | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| **Floor** | <0.02% | The IG → install funnel is broken; audit each step. |
| **Median** | 0.02–0.1% | Continue. |
| **Healthy** | 0.1–0.3% | Profitable organic acquisition. |
| **Hit** | >0.3% | Layer paid on top of the same Reels. |

At 0.2% e2e and ~1M cumulative views/month, we ship 2,000 unlocks/month = $14k/month net.

### 7. App Store keyword ranking

We track 5 keywords weekly:

- `lock in`
- `focus sounds`
- `study sounds`
- `rain sounds`
- `audio for locking in`

| State | Rank | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| **Floor** | not in top 100 | Re-shuffle keyword field; review velocity may be too low. |
| **Median** | top 30–100 | Continue; push reviews. |
| **Healthy** | top 10–30 | Maintain, push for top 10. |
| **Hit** | top 1–10 | Pure organic install firehose. |

## Vanity metrics we deliberately ignore

These are real metrics. We just don't make decisions on them.

- **Likes.** Useless. Saves > likes 100×.
- **Total followers.** Useful for credibility, not for revenue.
- **Reach.** Comes free with saves and shares. Don't optimize directly.
- **Comment count.** Quality matters, not quantity.
- **Story views.** Optimize by making 1 sticker poll/day; don't track aggregate.

## The Weekly Decision Meeting (with yourself, every Sunday Block A — 1pm)

15 minutes, every Sunday. Open the dashboard. Answer 5 questions:

1. **Which Reel had the highest save rate this week?** → Clone it 5 ways in the upcoming 7 days.
2. **Which Reel had the lowest save rate?** → Kill that format for 2 weeks.
3. **Was follower growth >2%/day?** → If yes, why? If no, why not? Adjust.
4. **Did at least one Reel produce ≥5 attributed unlocks?** → If yes, make sure 3 of next week's Reels echo its format.
5. **Are we missing a content pillar?** → Re-check the P1/P2/P3 ratio.

Write the answers in `Sheet 3: Monthly Strategy Notes` (see `06-DAILY-OPS-PLAYBOOK.md`).

## The Monthly Big Decisions

End of each month, ask:

### Month 1 (Day 30) decision points

- **Followers <2,000?** Brand voice or ICP is off. Re-read `01-POSITIONING-AND-ICP.md` and adjust.
- **No viral Reel signal?** Hook formulas may be too soft. Try the more aggressive POV/tier-list formats.
- **Unlocks <50?** App Store listing is failing. Re-do screenshots with a designer (cost: ~$300 on Fiverr or 2 days of own work).
- **Burnout risk red?** Hire the part-time editor now ($300/mo Upwork).

### Month 2 (Day 60) decision points

- **Followers <15k?** We're under-shipping. Increase Reels from 7/wk to 9/wk for 14 days.
- **Save rate stuck below 4%?** Quality issue. Audit recent Reels for hook strength. Watch them on a phone, sound off.
- **Install-to-unlock under 10%?** Paywall problem. A/B test new copy.
- **No 1 viral Reel yet?** Defer paid ads. Push 2 more weeks of organic. Don't rescue with money.

### Month 3 (Day 90) decision points

- **All targets hit?** Layer paid on top. Test influencer collab (1 collab, low-budget).
- **Below targets?** Diagnose: ICP wrong, content wrong, offer wrong, or product wrong. One of those four.

## The Kill List (formats we explicitly don't try unless we've earned them)

These look productive but reliably under-perform for our ICP:

- ❌ Influencer giveaways. (Brings the wrong audience.)
- ❌ "Day in my life" Reels. (Off-brand at month 1; revisit at month 6.)
- ❌ Trending dance audio. (Dilutes brand voice.)
- ❌ Long-form text carousels of "10 productivity tips." (Generic; doesn't ladder to identity.)
- ❌ Email gating before App Store. (Friction kills conversion.)
- ❌ Discount campaigns. (Brand-poison; we *raised* prices, never lower them.)

If you find yourself doing any of the above, stop. Re-read `01-POSITIONING-AND-ICP.md`.

## The Scale-Up Triggers

When *any* of these fire, scale aggressively:

| Trigger | Scale action |
|---|---|
| One Reel >500k views in <72hrs | Clear next 3 days. Ship 5 clones of that format. |
| One Reel >1M views | Cancel the rest of the week's plan. Ship 7 same-format Reels. Pitch press. |
| Save rate >7% on 3+ Reels in a week | Clone formats. Cancel underperformers. |
| Install-to-unlock >25% sustained | Run paid ads on the highest-saving Reels. $20/day. |
| Follower growth >5%/day for 7 days | Open a "founders" cohort enrollment. Limited to 1,000. Hike urgency. |
| App Store rank top-30 on `lock in` | Push press; we have a story. |

## The Revenue Math (sanity check)

We need to know: **how many Reel views = $1 of revenue?**

Assuming:
- e2e Reel-to-unlock rate at healthy median: 0.1%
- Unlock revenue (net of Apple's 30%): $7

Then: 1,000 Reel views = 1 unlock = $7. So **1 Reel view = $0.007 of revenue.**

Implications:
- A 100k-view Reel = ~100 unlocks = ~$700 net.
- A 1M-view Reel = ~1,000 unlocks = ~$7,000 net.
- The viral threshold (~500k) = ~$3,500 net per Reel.

This is the math we run when deciding to spend $300 on a designer or $500 on a part-time editor. Anything that lifts save rate by 10% lifts revenue 10%. ROI is straightforward.
